# NHK V3 Article SEO Projection Contract

> **NON-NORMATIVE.** This is a read/planning and public projection contract
> under the Constitution. SEO is not semantic truth and cannot create claims,
> relations, identities or evidence.

Status: approved contract, 2026-09-03. Article research supplies semantic
references, bounded evidence inventory, explainable related paths and
route-gated candidates; SEO remains read-only and cannot create semantic truth.

## Blueprint boundary

`ArticleSeoBlueprint` is derived planning data. It may contain user intent,
purpose, canonical subject reference, differentiated information gain,
title/H1/slug proposals, meta intent, outline, questions, internal-link and
media candidates, structured-data applicability, canonical/indexability
expectations, claim-compliance state and unresolved blockers.

It must not contain meta keywords, keyword-density targets or unsupported
facts. Vocabulary is natural Vietnamese and copy must be useful, accurate and
evidence-aware.

## Projection rules

Structured data, canonical tags, Open Graph and image sitemap output must use
the actual public page, visible content, canonical data and publicly eligible
assets. They must not fabricate ratings, reviews, product/video facts or
stronger claims than the evidence scope supports. Placeholder Media makes the
Article media/SEO stage incomplete and is excluded from preferred structured
data images and image sitemaps.

Title, H1, meta description and slug must be descriptive, differentiated,
stable, collision-checked and free of unsupported exaggeration. Internal links
must target public canonical routes; UUIDs, stable keys, private/retired
objects and technical routes are never emitted.

Public promotional/commercial claims use the shared
`PUBLIC_CLAIM_ADVERTISING_COMPLIANCE_CONTRACT.md` gate. Generated text is not
Evidence.

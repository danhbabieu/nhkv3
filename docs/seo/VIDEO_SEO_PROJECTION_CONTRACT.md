# Video SEO Projection Contract

SEO is generated from the canonical Video read/projection package after
semantic correctness and editorial completeness. A valid watch page has one
canonical URL, NHK title/H1, useful summary/body, description, indexability
decision, Open Graph fields and a `VideoObject` generated by
`VideoSeoProjection`.

All visitor-facing promotional/commercial Video and SEO copy is also subject to
`docs/compliance/PUBLIC_CLAIM_ADVERTISING_COMPLIANCE_CONTRACT.md`. This includes
NHK video title, summary, description, thumbnail text, SEO title/meta
Description, Open Graph text and other generated promotional projection. SEO
may rephrase existing truth for clarity, but it must not manufacture or conceal
an unsupported leadership, uniqueness or absolute-superiority claim.

`VideoObject` maps only available data: `name`, `description`, `thumbnailUrl`
when a stable HTTPS source thumbnail is allowed, `uploadDate`, ISO 8601
`duration`, `embedUrl` and canonical watch URL. Clips/key moments require
chapter/transcript evidence; timestamps are never guessed. Generated copy,
source-platform marketing text and AI confidence are not legal or semantic
evidence for an objective promotional claim.

The video sitemap is projection-only and includes active, indexable, available
Video watch pages that actually host the related video. Unavailable, private,
deleted, non-embeddable, placeholder, incomplete or compliance-blocked public
projection is excluded until the applicable publication gate is satisfied.
Native WordPress editorial sitemaps remain independent; unrelated Posts do not
receive Video sitemap entries.

## Sound quality

Pitch correctness and timbre realism are separate results:

- `PIANO_REFERENCE`: pitch-accurate for the chosen equal-tempered editorial
  octave, but low acoustic realism. The additive tone has no piano hammer,
  soundboard, pedal, room, microphone or measured instrument response.
- `BELL_SIMULATION`: pitch-accurate for the same event stream and recognizably
  bell-like, but not a measured tower-bell model. Its inharmonic partial ratios,
  attack and decay are synthetic assumptions and do not establish Big Ben,
  Cambridge or Westminster acoustic fidelity.

The current files are suitable as labelled notation demonstrations, not as
historical or high-fidelity instrument recordings.

## Rights and improved rendering path

The generated bytes use no external audio sample. The local synthesis code is
original research code, but public/commercial redistribution still requires a
governed rights decision. The Wikisource access page states CC BY-SA for its
text; attribution/share-alike and underlying-edition status require review.

For a higher-fidelity successor, the safest NHK-compatible options are either:

1. a commissioned original performance/render with explicit commercial,
   derivative and public-delivery rights; or
2. an original physical-model bell/piano render using documented parameters
   and source measurements whose data and code are redistributable.

A sample library that permits listening but not redistribution of rendered
   files is insufficient for governed public Media ingestion.

## Research questions that remain unresolved

The sources above do not by themselves establish the following runtime facts:

- an approved canonical score version, exact pitch classes, octaves, durations,
  tempo, tuning or segmentation;
- whether a proposed piano rendering is a transcription, arrangement or new
  performance, and whether its performer/recording rights are cleared;
- whether a bell simulation is an authorized derivative and whether it models
  the quarter bells, the Great Bell, or a different arrangement;
- the exact historical recording identity, chain of custody, checksum, license
  and permitted delivery route;
- a specific clock brand, model, variant or physical specimen that used the
  music, including the evidence boundary between historical Westminster use
  and a commercial clock implementation;
- public Media/MediaAsset delivery readiness for Piano, Bell Simulation or
  Historical Recording assets.
